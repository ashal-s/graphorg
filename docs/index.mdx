GraphOrg maps people, teams, departments, tools, technologies, services, infrastructure, documentation, ownership, and dependencies so new employees, consultants, and approved AI agents can understand the organisation before working.

This Mintlify site is the **public / browsable docs** surface. It is seeded from the repo `docs/` folder. Product UI lives in `apps/web`; full architecture and ADRs stay in Linear.
